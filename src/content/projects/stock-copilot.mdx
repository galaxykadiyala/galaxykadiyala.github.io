## The problem

Everyone online has their own take on when to buy, sell, or hold a stock —
and most of it is subjective noise with no clear reasoning behind it.

## The approach

Built a rule-based research tool reflecting a personal view grounded in
chart patterns and company financials, with the reasoning behind every call
shown transparently rather than hidden behind an opaque model.

## Decisions

- Deliberately rule-based instead of AI-driven — every recommendation
  traces back to an inspectable rule (trend, valuation, pullback signal),
  not a black-box prediction
- Surfaces the "why" alongside the verdict (e.g. trend, valuation, and dip
  signal shown together) instead of a single score with no context
- Built as a personal research tool first, reflecting one specific
  investing approach rather than trying to model every strategy

## Outcome

A working tool that takes any ticker and returns a clear, reasoned
entry/avoid recommendation grounded in trend and valuation signals — built
around one person's actual investing framework instead of a generic model.
