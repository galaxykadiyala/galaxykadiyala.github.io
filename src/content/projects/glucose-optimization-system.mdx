## The problem

Every CGM vendor app has its own view, and each is missing something. You
can't pinpoint a specific moment in the data. The views are cluttered with
features that aren't needed. None of them personalize anything, and food
logging is limited to whatever's built in — accurate tracking beyond that
isn't possible.

## The approach

Built a system combining CGM data with WhatsApp-based food logging (text or
photo) processed through the Claude API, so any meal can be logged in
seconds and matched against glucose response. It also works without a CGM
device — manual glucose readings can stand in when needed, since wearing a
CGM constantly isn't realistic but a manual check is always available.

## Decisions

- Chose WhatsApp + Claude API for food logging over a custom app UI, since
  texting a photo of a meal is faster than any manual entry form
- Built to work with or without continuous CGM data — manual glucose
  readings as a fallback, since no manual-reading option exists on the
  market today alongside CGM devices
- Prioritized personalization and point-in-time correlation (what caused
  *this* spike) over the broad, generic dashboards vendor apps ship with

## Outcome

![Glyco food intelligence view](/images/projects/glyco/food-intelligence.png)

A working system correlating meals to glucose response with specific,
actionable findings — like identifying chia seeds as the most effective
spike-reduction strategy, and refined carbohydrates as the top spike cause
— that generic CGM vendor apps don't surface.
