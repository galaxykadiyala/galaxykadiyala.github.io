## The problem

My daughter, starting primary school, enjoys math, logic, and painting
exercises. She'd joined Kumon, but it became too stressful for her — and
the online learning apps that could replace it were too costly for what
they offered.

## The approach

Built a learning platform using a Claude subscription and API instead of a
costly dedicated learning-app subscription, tailored to her actual level
and interests rather than a generic curriculum.

## Decisions

- Used an existing Claude subscription + API rather than paying for a
  dedicated learning app, making it far cheaper for the same (or more
  personalized) outcome
- Built exercises around her specific interests and current knowledge
  level instead of a fixed one-size-fits-all curriculum
- Optimized for low-stress engagement after Kumon's format proved too
  stressful for her

## Outcome

A platform she genuinely enjoyed using over the summer — math, logic, and
painting exercises she chose to do, at a fraction of the cost of
commercial alternatives.
